Compliance guardrails

Only a US person, on a US device, ever acts in the Gateway

Before we build: written legal sign-off on the Cockpit, and written confirmation that the Gateway's terms allow assisted form filling. If either is no, the desk stays at Model A's size.

Always

  • A named US operator signs in with their own credentials and MFA
  • The operator reviews every field and clicks submit personally
  • Lebanon sends bundles; the US sends back result files
  • Every session is logged and screen-recorded on the US device
  • A HIPAA BAA with every vendor that holds PHI

Never

  • Shared logins, or credentials stored on a server
  • Unattended submission without the Gateway's written permission
  • Screen sharing, remote control or mirroring of a session to Lebanon
  • Offshore staff seeing live Gateway screens
  • PHI in any tool without a BAA

Who and when

Any L2, L3 or L4 holding B2 can opt in; each night has at least one A2 holder as night lead. 16:00–24:00 Beirut (08:00–16:00 CT), Monday to Friday, 2 members a night. The rota is set each quarter from the opt-ins: 10 members at 1 night a week, up to 20 at 1 night every other week.

Duties

Bonus

A fixed $30,000 pool a quarter shared equally, $300–3,000 per member (about $230 per night worked). Full share when the rota hits 98% first pass, 15-minute answers and no deadline missed because of a bundle, and the member works all assigned nights; missed nights reduce it pro rata.
