Tests, reviews and badge loss

  • Quarterly test windows: last two weeks of March, June, September and December. Every badge test and the full skill assessment. Enrol up to 4 weeks before.
  • Testing out: people who already do the work can sit a badge test without the course, then 10 supervised cases.
  • Semiannual KPI review on June 30 and December 31: QA by badge, critical errors, work units and Net Recovery. A badge is lost below its QA keep threshold, with 2+ critical errors, or if suspended and not re-earned.
  • Losing 25% or more of badges held: 90 days of probation and coaching, re-test in the next window.
  • Losing 50% or more: termination through the HR process, with one appeal. Subject to HR and legal review under Lebanese labor law.
  • The level follows the badges and is reset at the next window; pay protection applies. June 2027 is coaching-only. Consequences start with the December 2027 review.

Transition: January to March 2027

  • December 2026: everyone ranked within their current role on 2026 KPIs. Default level: Seniors L3; Negotiators, Coders and Billers L2; Team Leads and Managers L5.
  • Top 10% of each role: earn the badges of their current role automatically, no tests, and are confirmed at their default level.
  • Bottom 10%: placed at L1 and enrolled in the Academy. They can return to L2 through any test window.
  • Everyone else: optional skill assessment in January, then 3 months (January 4 to March 31) to pass their role badges. Pass: confirmed. Fail: down one level. Pass the next level's tests: up one level.
  • April 1, 2027: levels take effect. Missing core badges are earned by June 30, 2027.
  • Pay: no one's pay goes down, including anyone moved down. Anyone moved up gets a guaranteed raise from April 1 (+10–12% or the band minimum, whichever is higher).

Our commitment: No one loses their job or pay because of the restructure. From December 2027 everyone is held to the same badge and performance standards.

  • Zero missed deadlines
  • Quality score
  • Work units against target
  • Ticket accuracy

Each team

  • Team Net Recovery
  • Days from service to final payment
  • Claims over 90 and 180 days
  • Awards paid within 30 days
  • Attrition

  • Disputes entered the same day
  • Minutes per dispute (target 15)
  • Copy errors under 0.5%
  • Backlog at end of day

Coding

  • Coding accuracy
  • Days from service to submission
  • NSA vs non-NSA classification errors
Net Recovery = (collected − initial payment − IDR fees not refunded) ÷ (billed − initial payment). Compared within the same portfolio tier.
Zero tolerance on deadlines. ClickUp counts every NSA deadline in business days and alerts the team lead, then the manager, three days before it's due.
